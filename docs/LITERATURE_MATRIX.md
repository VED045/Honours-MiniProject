## Use in the research paper

Cite the 2023 PeerJ review for the research landscape and the 2023 GAN survey for augmentation limitations. Cheah et al. is the closest fully accessible ANN/SMOTE empirical precedent, although its dataset differs from IEEE-CIS. Hayat and Magnier provides a methodological rationale for applying preprocessing and SMOTE only after splitting and for exposing precision–recall tradeoffs. Darwish et al. is an accessible same-dataset discussion with a different model family. Habibpour et al. is the closest DNN IEEE-CIS reference and is suitable for the uncertainty discussion, with the access/version caveats above.

The project should compare approaches on its own identical, untouched observations. Literature values must not be plotted beside project metrics as if they came from the same experiment. No claim of state-of-the-art performance, superiority over these papers, or reproduction is supported merely by citing them.

## Specific verification caveats

- The 2023 Habibpour journal version has eight authors according to both [UTS](https://opus.lib.uts.edu.au/handle/10453/177507) and [Deakin](https://dro.deakin.edu.au/articles/journal_contribution/Uncertainty-aware_credit_card_fraud_detection_using_deep_learning/22783271). Its [2021 preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2107.13508) has ten authors and a balanced subset; do not substitute preprint author metadata or unverified exact experimental numbers into the journal citation.
- Cheah et al. explicitly generates synthetic fraud records from training observations, but its Section 3.1 describes rescaling before splitting. The concern about scaler leakage is our interpretation of that stated sequence, not a reproduced finding.
- Darwish et al.'s IEEE-CIS Experiment 8 has incomplete replication details. It should inform contextual discussion and limitations, not establish a controlled numerical benchmark.
- The review by Marazqah Btoush et al. was published in 2023 and reviews 2019–2021 work. Its review-window dates must not be confused with its publication year.
- Some publisher HTML/PDF URLs could not be opened directly by the browser retrieval tool. Verification used indexed official publisher records, official PDF where available, PMC copies of publisher full text, and institutional metadata. No missing author, DOI or date was invented.
